OPIC STRATEGY TIPS
💡 How to Score High on OPIc
1
Speak in full sentences. Structured responses score higher.
2
Use connectors: First · Then · Finally · As a result
3
Add specific details and examples. Be vivid and concrete.
4
Practice daily. OPIc tests spontaneous speaking ability.
5
Don't memorize scripts. Use natural, spontaneous language.
